The morning train arrived at the station precisely twelve minutes behind schedule, as it often did on damp November mornings. Commuters shuffled along the platform with umbrellas folded tight against the persistent drizzle. Inside the small ticket office, a brass clock ticked rhythmically above the ticket counter, undisturbed by the hurried footsteps outside. I bought a warm cup of black tea and found a corner seat near the window, watching raindrops race down the pane as the carriage lurched into motion.
